I started where a lot of product people start without realizing it: making things people could see and use, not writing specs. Co-founding Arkade Media in Bengaluru meant learning positioning, client acquisition, and go-to-market by doing them for other people's businesses first: the fastest way I know to learn what actually moves a customer, before you're allowed to have opinions about roadmaps.

That grew into systems thinking. Freelance builds for Spectrum Infotech & Securities, VitaMind, and Foresight College of Commerce forced me to own a project end-to-end: discovery call, user flows, UX, and the full-stack build behind it, with AI-assisted development doing the heavy lifting where it earned its keep. As Founder's Associate at Point Of, that same instinct turned into growth execution: pitch decks, paid channels, and UX audits feeding straight into product decisions.

Today I'm the AI Product Manager behind ScoreVedaa at NeuraMach.AI, building an adaptive CAT-prep product from diagnostics to recommendation logic to a responsible-AI framework I actually believe in, because a model making decisions about a student's prep plan should have to earn that trust. Outside of product, I play basketball and run track; both taught me the same lesson product work keeps confirming: the score only ever reflects what you actually did in the possessions nobody was filming.
